HUNTINGTON, W.Va. – United States Attorney Moore Capito will address the media this afternoon at the Sidney L. Christie U.S. Courthouse and Federal Building in Huntington following the sentencing of Todd M. Chapman. Chapman, 58, of Huntington, pleaded guilty earlier to bank fraud, admitting that he embezzled at least $602,000 from a now-defunct Huntington law firm while its office manager. **WHO: United States Attorney Moore Capito** **WHAT: Media Availability** **WHEN: Thursday, February 26, 2026, at 3:00 p.m.** **WHERE:****Sidney L. Christie U.S. Courthouse and Federal Building845 Fifth Avenue, Huntington** ***** NOTE: the media availability is contingent upon the completion of the sentencing hearing, which is scheduled for 1:30 p.m. ***** A copy of this press release is located on the website of thehttps://www.justice.gov/usao/wvsfor the Southern District of West Virginia. **###**
