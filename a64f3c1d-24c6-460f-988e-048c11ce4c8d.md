## body

**NEW ORLEANS –**U.S. Attorney Duane A. Evans announced that on Tuesday, December 10, 2024,**ANGELA C. MITCHELL (“MITCHELL”)**, age 49, of Ponchatoula, Louisiana was sentenced to a year and a day in prison by U.S. District Judge Greg G. Guidry.**MITCHELL**had previously pleaded guilty to access device fraud in connection with her embezzlement from a Covington, Louisiana company ( “Company A” in court documents) where she worked from October 2016 to June 2018. According to court records,**MITCHELL**embezzled approximately $116,998.70 from Company A by, among other things, fraudulently transferring funds from Company A’s bank accounts via electronic transfers and by drafting unauthorized checks to herself.**MITCHELL**committed the fraud during her employment, and continued illegally accessing Company A’s accounts after she was terminated in June 2018. In addition to the term of imprisonment, Judge Guidry ordered**MITCHELL**to pay $116,998.70 in restitution to Company A. Judge Guidry also sentenced**MITCHELL**to three years of supervised release and ordered her to pay a $100 mandatory special assessment fee. U.S. Attorney Evans praised the work of the Homeland Security Investigations - New Orleans Field Office, in investigating this matter. Assistant U.S. Attorney Matthew R. Payne, of the Financial Crimes Unit, is in charge of the prosecution.
